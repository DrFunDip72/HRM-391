## Summary

Culture significantly influences organizational behavior and strategy, often overriding strategic plans. When organizations assume Western cultural traits will align with international operations, they may miss critical cultural differences, leading to ineffective strategies. Culture is embedded in internal environments, affecting how people interact, achieve goals, and function within organizations. Understanding culture's layers—artifacts, espoused values, and underlying assumptions—is crucial for aligning organizational strategy with cultural realities to avoid strategic failure.

## Key Points

- Culture eats strategy for breakfast, meaning organizational culture can override strategic plans if not aligned with the environment.
- Culture is embedded in the internal environment, influencing how people interact, achieve goals, and function within organizations.
- Organizational culture exists on three levels: artifacts (visible elements), espoused values (stated beliefs), and underlying assumptions (unconscious beliefs).
- Artifacts are observable elements like office space, dress codes, rituals, and policies that reflect organizational culture.
- Espoused values are the stated beliefs and values organizations claim to support, such as customer-first policies.
- Underlying assumptions are the deepest, often unconscious beliefs that drive behavior and are difficult to identify directly.
- Organizations may focus on external issues without addressing internal cultural issues that cause real problems.
- The culture iceberg model illustrates how artifacts are visible above water, while underlying assumptions lie deep beneath, influencing behavior without direct observation.
- Understanding these layers helps organizations address internal cultural issues that affect strategic execution and performance.
- Big takeaway — Culture significantly influences organizational behavior and strategy, often overriding strategic plans.

## Action Items

- Identify artifacts in your organization (e.g., dress code, office layout, rituals) by observing visible elements.
