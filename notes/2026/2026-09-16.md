## Key Points

- Charles framework: Spiritual (inner guidance), Physical (body/environment), Emotional (reaction/happiness), Contribution (impact over career), Intellectual (learning), Financial (resources), Interpersonal (relationships), Choices (time allocation)
- Contribution focuses on tangible impact rather than career position for adaptability and growth
- Task environment: Immediate interactions like suppliers or clients; general environment: broader external factors (political, economic, social, technological, environmental, legal)
- Critical dependency: High-impact reliance on a single supplier or factor that causes disruption if changed (e.g., Lightspeed software for a firm)
- Predictable change: Known shifts (e.g., sales doubling annually) that still require adaptation
- Environmental scan: Systematic process to identify, map, and analyze external factors for risk assessment
- Scene assessment: Evaluating broader context before acting to avoid premature fixes (medical triage analogy)
- Mentor board: Identify 1-2 mentors per life domain (spiritual, physical, intellectual) for support
- Default organization: Student's current workplace or role for practical environmental analysis exercises
